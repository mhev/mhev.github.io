Member support

Contact support@hevllc.com for account access, paid admission, privacy requests, reports, or moderation appeals.

For payment questions, include the receipt reference from Stripe. Never send a card number, password, or invitation code. If your payment is still being confirmed, do not pay again.

Returning members can choose Sign in without another invitation. To delete your account, open Profile → Account → Delete account. A deletion option is also available when a signed-in account has no membership or is suspended.

You can remove your map pin with Stop sharing, delete your posts from the feed menu, and report or block members from posts. For urgent moderation concerns, email support.

Malört Champion is operated by Hev LLC for adults 21 and older. Membership is available by invitation from an active member or one-time paid admission. Both routes provide the same access.
