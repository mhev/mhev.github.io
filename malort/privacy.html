Malört Champion
Privacy Policy

Malört Champion is operated by Hev LLC. Contact support@hevllc.com with privacy questions.

We collect your Apple account identifier, chosen display name and handle, membership number and admission history, and photos and captions you submit. If you choose to share location, we store rounded, neighborhood-scale coordinates and your city. You can browse the map without sharing, and stop sharing at any time to remove your stored pin.

Active members see approved posts, display names, membership announcements, and locations voluntarily shared on the map. Council moderators can review submitted photos and reports. Blocking hides members from each other in the app. Photos are kept in private storage and delivered using short-lived links. Recipients can still save or screenshot content you share.

We use Apple for sign-in, Supabase for authentication, database and photo storage, Apple Maps for maps, and Stripe for optional paid admission. Stripe processes your payment details; the app does not receive your full card number. We retain a payment reference and admission record. We do not sell personal data or use advertising trackers.

Profile information and posts are retained while your account exists. Delete individual posts from the feed, remove your map pin with Stop sharing, or delete your account in Account settings. Account deletion removes your profile, uploaded photos, posts, and associated personal content and revokes Apple sign-in access. Limited payment and fraud-prevention records may be retained where required for financial obligations; Stripe independently retains payment records under its policies. Service backups may retain copies until their normal retention period ends.

This club is for adults 21 and older. Do not submit information about minors. Contact support@hevllc.com for access, correction, privacy complaints, or questions about retained records. Material changes will be reflected in the app policy before new uses begin.

Effective September 4, 2026.
